Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Creating an account on ScrapStore is easy! Click on the user icon in the top right corner, then select "Sign Up". You can register with your email address or through your Google or Facebook account. Complete your profile with basic information, and you'll be ready to start buying or selling.

How does the escrow system work?

Our escrow system provides secure transactions. When you make a purchase:

  1. Your payment is held securely in escrow
  2. The seller ships the item
  3. You receive and inspect the item
  4. Funds are released to the seller only when you confirm satisfaction
What payment methods are accepted?

We accept various payment methods including:

  • Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
  • PayPal
  • Bank transfers
  • Crypto payments (Bitcoin, Ethereum)

All payments are processed securely through our payment gateway partners.

How do I return an item?

If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you can request a return within 30 days. Here's how:

  1. Go to your dashboard
  2. Find the order in your purchase history
  3. Click "Request Return"
  4. Explain the reason for return and upload photos if needed
  5. Wait for seller approval
  6. Ship the item back using trackable shipping
  7. Refund will be processed within 3-5 business days
What are the selling fees?

ScrapStore charges a 10% commission fee on all successful sales. This fee includes:

  • Listing and platform fees
  • Escrow service
  • Payment processing
  • Customer support

There are no upfront costs to list items for sale.

How do I contact a seller?

You can communicate with sellers through our built-in chat system. Simply:

  1. Go to the listing page
  2. Click the "Chat" button
  3. Send a message to the seller

Sellers are usually very responsive and can answer questions about the item's condition, shipping, or negotiate pricing.

How to Buy on ScrapStore

1. Find Your Part

Search for the specific part you need using our search bar or browse categories. Use filters to narrow down your options by condition, price, and location.

2. Contact Seller

Use our chat system to communicate directly with sellers. Ask questions about condition, shipping, and any other details before making a purchase.

3. Make Purchase

Complete your purchase using our secure escrow system. Your payment is held safely until you confirm you've received and inspected the item.

How to Sell on ScrapStore

1. Create Listing

Click "Sell" from the navigation menu. Fill out the listing form with details about your part, including photos, description, condition, and price.

2. Get Offers

Interested buyers will contact you through our chat system. Respond promptly to questions and negotiate pricing if needed.

3. Ship Item

Once a sale is agreed, ship the item promptly using trackable shipping. Update the buyer with shipping details and tracking number.

4. Get Paid

Funds are released to your account when the buyer confirms satisfaction. Withdraw your earnings to your bank account or PayPal.
